# Strive

Strive is a Java-based fitness tracker developed as part of our Object-Oriented Programming (OOP) 1 Capstone project. The app is designed to help users efficiently monitor their workouts, track fitness progress, and stay motivated to maintain consistency in their fitness journey.

With a focus on core OOP principles such as inheritance, encapsulation, and polymorphism, Strive is built to be a lightweight, user-friendly tool that helps users stay on top of their fitness goals. It integrates essential features like tracking exercise routines, monitoring workout streaks, and setting goals for future progress. Whether you’re a beginner or a seasoned athlete, Strive offers the right balance of simplicity and functionality to enhance your workout experience.

Key features of the app include:

• Personalized Profiles where users can input their details and fitness goals.\
• A weekly progression tracker that encourages consistency by tracking workouts and streaks.\
• An exercise suggestion menu that provides workout ideas with the option to customize.\
• A routine builder for users to plan their daily workouts.\
• A progress bar to visualize workout completion.\
• Goal setting for strength and fitness milestones to motivate users over time.\
• A fitness journal feature for users to track their physical and mental progress.\
• Workout history with chronological records of completed workouts and sets.

The app was developed with an emphasis on maintainability and extensibility using Java Swing for the user interface, making it easily adaptable to future updates and enhancements.

Strive is an educational project that demonstrates how object-oriented programming techniques can be effectively applied to real-world software solutions, specifically in the context of fitness and health. With a simple, intuitive design, Strive ensures that users can focus on their workouts without being overwhelmed by complex features or unnecessary distractions.
